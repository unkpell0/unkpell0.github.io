01 — About

How I work

I'm an Information Technology student at Politeknik Negeri Malang (Polinema), with hands-on freelance and internship experience at Six Zenith Company, where I worked across Java, PHP (Laravel), and AI automation tooling.

My approach to building software leans heavily on testing discipline: isolating components, reproducing bugs before fixing them, and asking "why" before accepting a quick patch. That habit is what pulled me toward QA-adjacent work — I like systems that are provably correct, not just systems that happen to work today.

Outside of coursework, I'm building a portfolio of AI-automation projects, with Onboarding Buddy as the centerpiece — a retrieval-augmented chatbot I debugged, tuned, and rebuilt end to end.
